Is my bus late?

Type the number on the front of your bus. You'll see every bus on that route in West Yorkshire right now, how late it is, and when it reaches the next stops.

Try , or .

    Positions come from the Department for Transport's live bus feed, about every 30 seconds, and are compared with the published timetable. On time means 1 minute early to 5 minutes late, the DfT's own window.
